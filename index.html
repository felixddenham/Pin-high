Pin High

A daily round of golf trivia. Nine holes, nine golf questions, par 36. Everyone plays the same course each day.

How to play
  • 🔎Rare-find holes. Name something that fits the prompt. The rarer your correct answer, the fewer shots.
  • 📍Closest-to-the-pin holes. Guess a number. The closer, the fewer shots.
  • 🚫Wrong answer = out of bounds. Play 3 off the tee: one more go with 2 shots already on the card. Miss again and you pick up with an 8.
  • 🔄Mulligan. Out of bounds? Spend a mulligan to replay the hole with no penalty. One per round.
  • ⏱️25 seconds a hole, 15 for a second go.
